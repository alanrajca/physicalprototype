Concept model · v0.4

FreshCam Prototype

A wire-free barcode & expiration-date scanner built on the Seeed XIAO ESP32S3 Sense. It wakes itself when you walk up with an item, then switches fully off between scans.

Changes in v0.4
• ESP32-CAM → XIAO ESP32S3 Sense (built-in USB-C charging)
• Charger board + boost converter removed
• Autofocus camera (OV5640/OV3660) for close-range barcodes
• Auto-wake: a small motion sensor (PIR) behind a black lens
• Scan button kept as a manual backup
• Added a white flash LED (the XIAO doesn't have one)
• Same 40 × 60 × 27 mm shell, magnets and mounting options

Battery life (estimate)

~2–4 months per charge
With auto-wake on: 10 scans a day plus ~40 false wake-ups from people walking past. Button-only use could last 6+ months.
Motion sensor, always on (≤0.1 mA)≤2.4 mAh/day
False wake-ups (~3 s, no Wi-Fi)~4 mAh/day
Real scans (~8 s incl. Wi-Fi upload)~5.5 mAh/day
Total vs ~1,300 mAh usable~12 mAh/day

Why there's still a power switch: the XIAO chip sleeps at ~14 µA, but with the camera attached it draws ~3 mA in sleep, and users report ~90 mA after the camera has been used. So the circuit cuts power completely, and the motion sensor or button turns it back on.

Design specs

  • Board: Seeed XIAO ESP32S3 Sense, 21 × 17.5 × 15 mm with camera board
  • Camera: OV5640 autofocus (current shipments; some ship OV3660)
  • Battery: 3.7 V 1500 mAh LiPo (803450, protected), wired to the XIAO's battery pads through the power switch
  • Charging: XIAO's own USB-C at ~100 mA, so a full charge takes about 15–18 hours
  • Wake: AM312 mini PIR sensor or button → power switch on → scan → upload → off
  • Wi-Fi: flexible antenna on the inside of the front shell, away from the battery
  • Magnets: 6× N52, 12 × 3 mm (left, right, back) behind rubber grip pads
  • Enclosure: 40 × 60 × 27 mm matte black PETG/ASA, 2-part snap-fit
