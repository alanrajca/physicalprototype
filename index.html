Concept model · v0.3

FreshCam Prototype

A wire-free ESP32-CAM barcode & expiration-date scanner in a matte black shell, with magnets on three sides so it can mount wherever works best.

Changes in v0.3
• Internal 1500 mAh battery, so no cable to the wall
• USB-C port on top is now for charging only
• Front scan button wakes the device to save battery
• Magnets on the left, right and back (6 total)
• 4 mounting options, including inside the fridge

Battery life (estimate)

~1,000–2,000 scans per charge
About 3–6 months at ~10 scans a day. The device is fully powered off between scans: pressing the button turns it on, it scans, uploads over Wi-Fi, then cuts its own power.
This requires the power-latch circuit. If the ESP32-CAM only uses its built-in sleep mode instead, the battery lasts roughly 1–3 weeks.

Design specs

  • Enclosure: 40 × 60 × 27 mm, 2-part snap-fit, matte black PETG/ASA
  • Battery: 3.7 V 1500 mAh LiPo (803450, protected), USB-C charging
  • Magnets: 6× N52, 12 × 3 mm, two each on the left, right and back, behind rubber grip pads so pressing the button doesn't slide it
  • Wake: front button turns the unit on through a power-latch circuit; it switches itself off after each scan
  • Scan distance: ~15–30 cm in front of the lens, flash LED for low light
  • Board: ESP32-CAM with pin headers removed, wires soldered directly so the battery fits behind it

The wall adapter and long cable are gone. Charge it with any USB-C phone charger.

Worth considering for v0.4: a Seeed XIAO ESP32S3 Sense board (~$14–20) has a camera, built-in battery charging and much lower sleep power. It could replace the charger board and the boost converter, and make it practical for the unit to wake automatically.
